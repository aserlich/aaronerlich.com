# Registered reports published in political science journals

Working list for adjudication. Built from OpenAlex full-text search for the exact
phrase "registered report", filtered by journal ISSN — the only method that works,
since Crossref carries no article-type field for these journals and no journal
publishes a count.

**Tick the box if the paper IS a registered report** (two-stage review with in-principle
acceptance), not merely a preregistered study or a paper citing one. Every registered
report has a pre-analysis plan, so registration language alone does not settle it.

## Known gap

Self-identification undercounts. The *JEPS* paper *How Language Shapes Belief in
Misinformation* ([https://doi.org/10.1017/xps.2025.10011](https://doi.org/10.1017/xps.2025.10011)) is a registered report and
says so nowhere in its title or abstract — it is in the adjudication list above only
because the phrase appears in its full text. Any registered report whose full text is
not indexed is invisible to this method entirely, so these counts are lower bounds.
